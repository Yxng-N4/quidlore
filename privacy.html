Privacy Policy

Last updated 23 October 2026

Quidlore ("we") makes short educational videos explaining how businesses make money and uses an in-house publishing tool (the "App") to upload those videos to Quidlore's own accounts on YouTube, TikTok and Instagram. This policy explains what data the App handles.

Who is responsible

Quidlore is run by Afa Nfor in the United Kingdom. Contact: afanyu.nfor@gmail.com.

What the App accesses

The App only signs in to accounts owned by Quidlore. When we connect an account, the platform (Google/YouTube, TikTok or Meta/Instagram) gives the App an access token and basic profile details for that account (account ID, name, handle). The App uses these only to upload and publish our videos and read their performance statistics (views, likes, followers).

The App does not collect data about viewers or other users, does not read private messages, and does not sell, rent or share data with anyone.

Google API Services

The App's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. YouTube data is handled under the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Storage and security

Access tokens are stored encrypted-at-rest on a private computer controlled by Quidlore and are never published. Video files are stored only as long as needed to publish them.

Retention

Tokens are kept until the account is disconnected. Performance statistics are kept for up to 24 months for our own reporting.

Data deletion

To have any data held by the App deleted, email afanyu.nfor@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion". We will delete it within 30 days and confirm by email. You can also revoke access at any time from your Google, TikTok or Meta account settings, which immediately stops the App using it.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you can ask to access, correct or delete your data, or complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

Changes

We will update this page if anything changes; the date above shows the latest version.
